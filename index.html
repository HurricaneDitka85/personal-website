At a Glance

About Sean

Who is Sean M. Hogan?

Sean M. Hogan is a Denver, Colorado operating executive. He served concurrently as COO, CFO and CPO of Alpha USA, a national faith-based organizational network, where he led a financial turnaround, grew the partner base from about 5,000 to about 10,000 organizations, and launched a separate corporate entity. He now serves as vCIO (Systems Strategy) on the Gloo360 team at Gloo.

What roles is Sean open to?

CEO and president roles, particularly at early-stage and growth-stage technology ventures that need one leader who can own strategy, finance, product and operations together. He also takes on select board and advisory work.

What is Sean's background?

Sean began his career in consulting at Accenture and as a program manager at Microsoft (through Logic 20/20), then held concurrent C-suite roles at Alpha USA and served as fractional CEO of AIUSA Inc. He co-founded Dynasty Decoder, a sports analytics software platform. He holds an MA in Counseling Psychology, a BS in Business Systems from Taylor University, and is a Doctor of Business Administration candidate at Durham University.

Where is Sean based?

The Denver, Colorado area. He works with organizations nationally.
